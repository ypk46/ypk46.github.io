2048 Reloaded Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

2048 Reloaded ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Yuyi Kimura ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the app stores, what data its advertising provider collects, and the choices you have.

Data the app stores on your device

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • your current game, so you can continue where you left off;
  • your leaderboards (your top games, each with its score, highest tile, move count and date);
  • your settings, such as sound, haptics and theme;
  • your ad consent choice, where one is required (see Your choices).

This data never leaves your device. I don't collect it, and the app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. You can reset your leaderboards in Settings, and you can delete all of this data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The app is free and shows optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. The ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is the only part of the app that uses the internet. The game itself plays fully offline.

To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device and other identifiers, such as the advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • app interactions, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostics, such as crash and performance data about the ads SDK.

This data is sent to and processed by Google, encrypted in transit. I don't receive it in a form that identifies you. To learn how Google uses it, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before it requests any ad. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices, which appears wherever consent applies.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • No ads at all. Ads only play when you choose to watch one. You can play the whole game without watching any.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and their data was collected by the ads SDK, contact me and I will help with your request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
